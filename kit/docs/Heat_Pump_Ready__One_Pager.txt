[page] Heat Pump Ready? One-Pager
    Track: Buildings & Everyday Energy · Challenge partner: nuuEnergy
  [sub_header] The problem
  Millions of homes still heat with gas or oil. Heat pumps are the cleaner alternative and work in far more existing buildings than people think. Yet most homeowners never get past the first question: "Would a heat pump even work in my home?" Today, only a technician's visit answers it.
  [sub_header] Your challenge
  Build a tool that gives a homeowner an honest first answer in under three minutes, using only what they already have: their energy bill, a few facts about the building and some smartphone photos.
  The result must tell the homeowner:
  [bulleted_list] Ready or not? Green / yellow / red, with a reason they understand
  [bulleted_list] What's still open? What the tool can't tell, and what a technician has to check on site
  How you get there is up to you.
  [sub_header] What you get
  [bulleted_list] 5 real example buildings (CSV + JSON): 3 come with nuuEnergy's reference values to build and calibrate on, for 2 the jury keeps the reference values hidden
  [bulleted_list] Fixed assumptions, so all cost comparisons are comparable:
    Value | Assumption
    26 ct/kWh | Heat-pump electricity
    11 ct/kWh | Natural gas
    1.70 €/litre (≈ 17 ct/kWh) | Heating oil
    4.0 underfloor heating · 3.5 radiators up to 55 °C · 2.8 radiators 55–60 °C | Heat-pump efficiency (JAZ)
